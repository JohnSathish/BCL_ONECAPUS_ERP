Welcome speech (2–3 minutes)

Stand, smile, pause once after the first paragraph. Do not click yet.

Good morning / Good afternoon. Thank you for your time. Today I will walk you through OneCampus — a complete College ERP built for day-to-day operations under NEP 2020 and FYUGP, from admission to alumni.

Most colleges today run on a mix of registers, spreadsheets, and separate softwares for fees, exams, and attendance. That creates duplicate work, delayed reports, and weak audit trails when NAAC or the university asks for evidence. OneCampus replaces that fragmentation with one shared student and staff record across admissions, academics, examinations, fees, HR, library, and governance.

You will see four perspectives: the public college face and admissions entry; the Admin ERP where the office runs the institution; the Faculty portal for teaching work; and the Student portal and mobile app for self-service. I will keep explanations brief and show the menus as we go. Please stop me anytime with questions.

Act A — Website, ERP, and Admissions

“Before we log in, notice three surfaces. The college website is the public face — news, about the college, programmes. The ERP is the secure operations system for staff and students. Admissions is the applicant entry point that feeds directly into the ERP — no retyping of applications.”
Click path Open public site → then ERP login → optionally Admissions portal apply page
Show
  • College website home (marketing / information)
  • ERP login (tenant / institution, role-based access)
  • Admissions portal as the student-facing apply & pay flow

Why it matters: One brand outside; one database inside — applicants become students without Excel handoffs.

Act B — Admin: Dashboard, Students, Admissions

B1 · Dashboard

“This is the Admin home. Leadership sees institution-wide activity without chasing departments for spreadsheets. Every other user only sees menus their role allows.”

B2 · Students lifecycle

“Every enrolled learner lives here — directory, profile, academic status, fees link, and verification. From application to promotion, it is one continuous record.”
Click path Students → Student Directory / Add Student / Subject Registration / Student Profile Verification / Promotion

B3 · Admissions

Act K — Closing speech

To close: OneCampus is one platform for the whole campus lifecycle — public admissions in, administration in the middle, and faculty plus students served on web and mobile.

What you saw today is not a brochure of separate modules. Admissions feed Students; curriculum feeds Timetable, LMS, and Exams; fees and certificates read the same records; governance and NAAC draw evidence from daily work.

I am happy to take questions — on rollout, training, data migration, NEP subject renewal, or any module you want to see again in depth. Thank you.
