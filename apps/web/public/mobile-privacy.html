DBC Student App — Privacy Policy

Institution: Don Bosco College, Tura (Meghalaya)

Last updated: July 2026

This policy describes how the DBC Student mobile app (BCL OneCampus ERP) collects, uses, and protects information when you sign in with your college student account.

Information we collect

  • Account identifiers you use to sign in (email)
  • Student profile and academic data shown in the app (fees, attendance, notifications)
  • Device identifier for session security and fraud prevention
  • Payment transaction references when you pay fees through the app

How we use information

  • Authenticate you and keep your session secure
  • Display your fees, attendance, and college notifications
  • Process fee payments through Razorpay (payment partner)
  • Improve reliability and provide technical support

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Payment data is handled by Razorpay under their privacy policy. We share data only as needed to operate the service for Don Bosco College Tura or as required by law.

Retention & security

Data is stored on servers operated for the college. Access tokens are stored securely on your device. Contact the college administration to request account changes or data-related questions.
