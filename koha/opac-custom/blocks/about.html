About Don Bosco College Library

The Don Bosco College Library, Tura is a learning resource centre committed to supporting the academic, research, and personal growth of our students, faculty, and the wider community. The library provides access to a rich collection of books, journals, e-resources, and digital materials in a calm and welcoming environment.

We strive to create a space where knowledge meets inspiration, and where every student can explore new ideas, build skills, and develop into responsible global citizens, in the spirit of Don Bosco’s vision of education.

Our Vision

“To be a dynamic learning resource centre that empowers individuals to discover, create and share knowledge for a better and more humane world.”

Our Mission

“To provide equitable access to quality information resources and services, support teaching, learning and research, and foster a culture of reading and lifelong learning in the spirit of Don Bosco.”

Library Rules

  • Carry your college / library identity card and present it when asked.
  • Keep silence in the reading room so that others can study in peace.
  • Do not take bags, food, or uncovered drinks into the stack and reading areas.
  • Handle books and journals with care. Marking, folding, or tearing pages is not allowed.
  • Return or renew items on or before the due date to avoid overdue fines.
  • Personal belongings are kept at your own risk. Use the property counter where provided.
  • Follow the instructions of library staff. The librarian may refuse entry or borrowing for misuse of the library.

Library Team

Our librarians and support staff help you search the catalogue, locate books, renew loans, and use e-resources such as N-LIST and the National Digital Library.

For assistance, please come to the circulation desk or write to library@donboscocollege.ac.in.

Facilities & Services

  • Circulation: check-out, check-in, and renewal of books
  • Reading room of about 1,300 sq. ft. for quiet study
  • Reference collection and question banks
  • Photostat and internet access for academic use
  • Access to N-LIST, National Digital Library, and other e-resources
  • Online catalogue (OPAC) to search, reserve, and renew

FAQ

How do I log in to the catalogue?
Use your library card number or username and the password given at the circulation desk.

How many books can I borrow?
Loan limits depend on your patron category. Check your account after login, or ask at the desk.

Can I renew online?
Yes. Log in, open your account, and renew eligible items before they become overdue.
